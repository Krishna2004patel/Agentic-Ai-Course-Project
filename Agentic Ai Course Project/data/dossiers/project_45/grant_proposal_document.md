## 1. Executive Summary
This investigation addresses urgent scientific and technical challenges in Neuromorphic edge computing using memristive crossbar arrays for micro-UAV navigation within the domain of Nanoelectronics & Autonomous Robotics. Existing methodologies suffer from significant operational bottlenecks, empirical latency, and vulnerability to operational drift. Under the leadership of Dr. Prateek Roy at IIT Kharagpur, this 16-week grant project deploys a systematic four-phase investigative methodology designed to establish measurable performance breakthroughs, verifiable reproducibility, and statutory grant compliance.

## 2. Technical Methodology
The proposed methodology executes an end-to-end, empirical multi-stage research lifecycle: (Phase 1: Theoretical Modeling & Formulation) Formulate rigorous analytical representations and baseline telemetry protocols for Neuromorphic edge computing using memristive crossbar arrays for micro-UAV navigation. (Phase 2: Prototype & System Architecture) Design and implement modular prototype units integrating real-time sensory perception, algorithmic processing, and error-bounding guardrails. (Phase 3: Characterization & Stress Validation) Subject the integrated system to high-dimensional operational perturbations, cross-validating empirical findings against prior art. (Phase 4: Field Validation & Deployment) Conduct pilot validation in target experimental environments at IIT Kharagpur, accompanied by automated compliance auditing and artifact preservation.
