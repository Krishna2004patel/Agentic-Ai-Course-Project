## 1. Executive Summary
This research proposal presents a novel agentic methodology to address fundamental challenges in Deep-Sea Autonomous Underwater Vehicle Telemetry and Swarm Mapping. Operating out of National Institute of Ocean Technology, the project unites automated multi-agent coordination with verifiable domain guardrails, achieving measurable improvements over current industry benchmarks. The initiative directly targets core objectives in Marine Robotics & Ocean Engineering by deploying verifiable experimental pipelines.

## 2. Technical Methodology
The methodology combines hierarchical agent orchestration with retrieval-augmented generation and deterministic tool validation. Agent roles are segmented into perception, planning, tool-assisted action, and automated safety verification.
