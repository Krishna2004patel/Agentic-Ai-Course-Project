## 1. Executive Summary
This research proposal presents a novel agentic methodology to address fundamental challenges in Autonomous UAV Precision Agriculture and Early Crop Disease Detection. Operating out of Punjab Agricultural University, the project unites automated multi-agent coordination with verifiable domain guardrails, achieving measurable improvements over current industry benchmarks. The initiative directly targets core objectives in Smart Agriculture & Drone Tech by deploying verifiable experimental pipelines.

## 2. Technical Methodology
The proposed methodology incorporates a four-stage precision agritech pipeline: (1) In-situ multispectral foliar data acquisition and micro-climate soil telemetry; (2) Preprocessing with illumination-invariant contrast enhancement; (3) Edge-optimized deep convolutional and vision-transformer neural architectures; and (4) Automated multi-agent advisory synthesis with farmer-facing decision alerts.
