## 1. Executive Summary
This research proposal presents a novel agentic methodology to address fundamental challenges in AI-based early disease detection and predictive biomarker screening. Operating out of KLE Technological University, the project unites automated multi-agent coordination with verifiable domain guardrails, achieving measurable improvements over current industry benchmarks. The initiative directly targets core objectives in Biomedical AI by deploying verifiable experimental pipelines.

## 2. Technical Methodology
The methodology combines clinical signal acquisition, automated feature normalization, and deep neural biomarker representation. Multi-agent supervision layers audit confidence scores and flag out-of-distribution cases for human clinical review.
