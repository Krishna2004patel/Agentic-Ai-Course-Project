## 1. Executive Summary
This research proposal presents a novel agentic methodology to address fundamental challenges in Smart Fiber-Optic Sensor Networks for Infrastructure and Construction Safety. Operating out of KLE Technological University, the project unites automated multi-agent coordination with verifiable domain guardrails, achieving measurable improvements over current industry benchmarks.

## 2. Technical Methodology
The methodology combines hierarchical agent orchestration with retrieval-augmented generation and deterministic tool validation. Agent roles are segmented into perception, planning, tool-assisted action, and automated safety verification.
